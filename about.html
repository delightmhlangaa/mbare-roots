About Mbare Roots

Mbare Roots was born in the heart of Harare's oldest township - Mbare. We are not a big company. We are sons and daughters of Mbare who want to share our real life with visitors.

When you walk with us, you don't just see Mbare Musika market, you meet Mai Chipo who sells mazhanje, you hear the drums from our community hall, you taste sadza and muriwo cooked on firewood.

Our Story

Started in 2023 by Tinashe, a local youth who saw tourists only going to Victoria Falls but never seeing real township life. Mbare has history - from Mbare Musika to Rufaro Stadium. It has music - sungura, dancehall, mbira. It has hustle and heart.

What Makes Us Different
